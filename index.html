Frequently asked questions

Do I have to give up foods I love?

No. Plans can incorporate familiar foods, preferences and cultural eating patterns while working toward agreed goals.

Do you work with medical conditions?

Nutrition support may be available for PCOS, diabetes, thyroid conditions and cholesterol concerns. Care should be coordinated with your doctor, and scope will be confirmed before booking.

Can we work online?

Yes. Consultations are provided online, with initial enquiries handled through WhatsApp or email.

How soon will I see results?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Progress depends on your health, goals, starting point and consistency. Your plan will be reviewed and adjusted using appropriate measures.

Your health information, handled carefully

Share only the minimum information needed to arrange an initial conversation. Detailed medical history and reports should be discussed during your private online consultation.

Privacy

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message containing your name, phone number and selected goal. You can review or edit it before choosing Send. Do not include reports or sensitive medical details in the initial message.

Medical note

Website information and calculator estimates are general education, not diagnosis or emergency care. Always follow advice from your treating clinician.
